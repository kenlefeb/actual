# Release 26.10.1-alpha.14 — Ken’s Actual fork

October 28, 2026

**Unofficial fork prerelease.** Based on Actual 26.10.0 (`2bebdbaae0b701120a8cad9d56aafd64fa04c577`); not an official Actual Budget release.

Paychecks get their own experimental setting, and the paycheck editor shows year-to-date totals and lets you change how early deposits arrive.

Docker Tag: `ghcr.io/kenlefeb/actual-hosting:run-<successful workflow run>-1` (private; see the accompanying `image.ref`, `image.id` and `source.json` for exact provenance).

#### Features

- The paycheck editor shows a year-to-date summary for this year and last: gross pay, each deduction and net pay, from posted transactions.

#### Enhancements

- Paychecks have their own "Paychecks" experimental setting. Turning it on also turns on business calendars. Budgets that already use business calendars or have a paycheck keep paychecks on.
- Each deposit's business days early can be changed in the paycheck editor. The change applies to every paycheck that deposits to that account, and the editor says so.

#### Bugfixes

- No new fork bugfixes in this release; earlier fixes are retained.

#### Maintenance

- Tests cover the new setting, the days-early field and the year-to-date totals.

#### Known limitations

- Experimental; the paycheck editor is desktop only.
- "Upcoming dates" in schedule and paycheck editors does not reflect skipped dates.
- Each deposit must go to a different account, and every paycheck repeats its defined amounts; edit a posted transaction for a one-off change.
- Imported deposits more than about 7.5% from the expected amount do not link automatically.
- Releases published on the same day may show the "What's new" message for any of them; the Notifications page lists all.
- Synthetic staging only; no real financial data or production paperwork migration. No iPhone acceptance test is claimed.
