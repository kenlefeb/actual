# Release 26.10.1-alpha.15 — Ken’s Actual fork

October 29, 2026

**Unofficial fork prerelease.** Based on Actual 26.10.0 (`2bebdbaae0b701120a8cad9d56aafd64fa04c577`); not an official Actual Budget release.

Paychecks get their own section on the Schedules page and can be edited on a phone, clearing accounts warn when they don't empty, holidays can follow Easter, and business calendars can be managed on a phone.

Docker Tag: `ghcr.io/kenlefeb/actual-hosting:run-<successful workflow run>-1` (private; see the accompanying `image.ref`, `image.id` and `source.json` for exact provenance).

#### Features

- The Schedules page has a Paychecks section at the top, on desktop and mobile. Each paycheck shows its next pay date, net pay and where each deposit goes. You can skip the next paycheck (and undo it), see skipped dates, open a paycheck to edit it, or add one. A paycheck's own schedules no longer clutter the schedules list.
- Paychecks can be created, edited, skipped and deleted on a phone, on a single scrolling page with net pay and Save always in view.
- Business calendars can be managed on a phone: the Schedules page links to a list of calendars, and each calendar opens in a full-page editor. Deleting a calendar asks first.
- Business calendar holidays can be set relative to Easter Sunday (for example, Good Friday is 2 days before), so they no longer need a new exception every year.

#### Enhancements

- An account that a paycheck uses for clearing shows a paycheck icon in the sidebar and a note at the top of its register saying it should return to zero after each payday.
- When a clearing account still has a balance and a deposit is more than 2 business days late, its register and the paycheck's row on the Schedules page warn, with the balance and the deposits that haven't arrived.
- The Easter offset field explains when its value isn't a whole number from -100 to 100 and won't save until it is.

#### Bugfixes

- A paycheck posted before its pay date now shows as paid instead of "Skipped".

#### Maintenance

- Sync server dependencies updated to fix known security vulnerabilities (express, express-rate-limit, handlebars and related packages).
- Accessibility improvements in the Paychecks section, the business calendar year preview and the mobile sync status.
- Pull requests and the development branch are scanned with Semgrep.

#### Known limitations

- Experimental.
- "Upcoming dates" in schedule and paycheck editors does not reflect skipped dates.
- Each deposit must go to a different account, and every paycheck repeats its defined amounts; edit a posted transaction for a one-off change.
- Imported deposits more than about 7.5% from the expected amount do not link automatically.
- If two paychecks are both posted before the first one's pay date, the second may show as skipped.
- The mobile business calendar editor uses the desktop editor's layout.
- Releases published on the same day may show the "What's new" message for any of them; the Notifications page lists all.
- Synthetic staging only; no real financial data or production paperwork migration. No iPhone acceptance test is claimed.
