# Release 26.10.1-alpha.17 — Ken’s Actual fork

October 29, 2026

**Unofficial fork prerelease.** Based on upstream Actual `master` at `21c2b3f703142f858af4c653a31e675ca5ff85d7` (October 26, 2026), which comes after Actual 26.10.0 and is not itself an upstream release; not an official Actual Budget release.

This release brings in four weeks of upstream Actual changes made after 26.10.0, including security fixes for bank sync credentials. A new pay schedule no longer counts older paychecks linked to it, so its skipped paydays show again.

Docker Tag: `ghcr.io/kenlefeb/actual-hosting:run-<successful workflow run>-1` (private; see the accompanying `image.ref`, `image.id` and `source.json` for exact provenance).

#### Features

- From upstream: choose which pages appear in the sidebar, their order and the page Actual opens on; sync linked bank accounts automatically at a chosen interval; group custom reports by tags; investment phases for Monte Carlo pots; the Porcelain theme; and South African Rand in the currency picker.

#### Enhancements

- The API's schedule dates (`getScheduleDates`) also work for a date relative to another schedule.
- From upstream: 32 enhancements, including a richer result from the API's `runBankSync`. The Experimental settings use upstream's new layout; the Business calendars and Paychecks settings are unchanged.

#### Bugfixes

- A new or re-dated pay schedule no longer counts older paychecks linked to it, so its first skipped paydays show as "Skipped".
- From upstream: 73 bug fixes, including keeping sign-in and bank connection credentials out of server request logs, preventing bank sync credentials from being reused after a budget is deleted, not leaving a budget file half-written when saving fails, and posting a schedule created or edited later on the day it is due.

#### Maintenance

- From upstream: dependency updates, Node.js 24.22.1 and stricter React Compiler lint rules. Three fork hooks were reworked to follow those rules.
- Tests cover relative dates in the API's schedule dates, and paychecks with history from before their pay schedule started.

#### Known limitations

- Experimental.
- Upstream changes made after 26.10.0 have not yet been through an upstream release.
- "Upcoming dates" in schedule and paycheck editors does not reflect skipped dates.
- Each deposit must go to a different account, and every paycheck repeats its defined amounts; edit a posted transaction for a one-off change.
- Imported deposits more than about 7.5% from the expected amount do not link automatically.
- A duplicate of the latest paycheck (for example, imported twice) can hide the next skipped date.
- The mobile business calendar editor uses the desktop editor's layout.
- Releases published on the same day may show the "What's new" message for any of them; the Notifications page lists all.
- Synthetic staging only; no real financial data or production paperwork migration. No iPhone acceptance test is claimed.
