# Release 26.10.1-alpha.18 — Ken’s Actual fork

October 29, 2026

**Unofficial fork prerelease.** Based on upstream Actual `master` at `21c2b3f703142f858af4c653a31e675ca5ff85d7` (October 26, 2026), which comes after Actual 26.10.0 and is not itself an upstream release; not an official Actual Budget release.

This release fixes a way to crash the sync server before signing in, and several schedule problems: schedules dated relative to another schedule now move on after they're paid or posted, goal templates budget every occurrence of them, paychecks show their upcoming and skipped dates again, and Business calendars stays on while you have paychecks.

Docker Tag: `ghcr.io/kenlefeb/actual-hosting:run-<successful workflow run>-1` (private; see the accompanying `image.ref`, `image.id` and `source.json` for exact provenance).

#### Features

- None in this release.

#### Enhancements

- Choose an account's business calendar on a phone, as on desktop.
- Business calendars can't be turned off while the budget has paychecks, because paychecks use them; a note in Settings > Experimental explains why. With no paychecks, turning Business calendars off also turns Paychecks off. If your budget has paychecks but business calendars are turned off, Paychecks now starts off too. Turn on Paychecks in Settings > Experimental to see them again.

#### Bugfixes

- A specially crafted compressed request can no longer crash the sync server before anyone signs in.
- Schedule previews and next dates update as soon as a business calendar change syncs in from another device.
- A schedule dated relative to another schedule moves to its next date after it is paid or automatically posted, instead of staying on the old date. Schedules stuck on alpha.17 catch up the first time the budget opens: any occurrences missed since then are posted. Check for deposits you entered yourself meanwhile.
- A goal template for a schedule dated relative to another schedule budgets every occurrence in the month, not just one.
- Paychecks show "Show more" and their skipped dates again, rules name the schedule a relative date follows, and screens that move dates to business days use the right calendar.

#### Maintenance

- Tests cover relative dates in goal templates and forecasts, schedule business-day scenarios, and paycheck frequency changes, same-day holiday deposits and the hidden menu.
- The client version test finds the version in the redesigned Settings.
- Fork checks build the app before changes merge.

#### Known limitations

- Experimental.
- Upstream changes made after 26.10.0 have not yet been through an upstream release.
- "Upcoming dates" in schedule and paycheck editors does not reflect skipped dates.
- Each deposit must go to a different account, and every paycheck repeats its defined amounts; edit a posted transaction for a one-off change.
- Imported deposits more than about 7.5% from the expected amount do not link automatically.
- A duplicate of the latest paycheck (for example, imported twice) can hide the next skipped date.
- The mobile business calendar editor uses the desktop editor's layout.
- Goal templates already applied under alpha.17 keep their amounts until templates are applied again.
- Releases published on the same day may show the "What's new" message for any of them; the Notifications page lists all.
- Synthetic staging only; no real financial data or production paperwork migration. No iPhone acceptance test is claimed.
