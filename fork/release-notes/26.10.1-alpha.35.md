# Release 26.10.1-alpha.35 — Ken’s Actual fork

November 13, 2026

**Unofficial fork prerelease.** Based on upstream Actual v26.11.0 (`1766241e3f4b3c578e424c4fa15f1eb83c0da17d`, October 29, 2026); not an official Actual Budget release.

This release makes the sync server delete documents that were removed from transactions, after a waiting period, instead of keeping them forever. It also adds a native Budget tab to the iPhone app and an MCP tool that lists a transaction's documents.

Docker Tag: `ghcr.io/kenlefeb/actual-hosting:run-<successful workflow run>-1` (private; see the accompanying `image.ref`, `image.id` and `source.json` for exact provenance).

#### Features

- The sync server deletes a document 30 days after a device first reports it removed from its transaction. Undoing the removal on any device, or opening the document, restarts the 30 days. A deleted budget's documents are deleted 30 days after the budget was deleted. A daily job does the deleting, at most 500 documents per run, and logs each deletion. Set `ACTUAL_ATTACHMENTS_ORPHAN_RETENTION_DAYS` to change the waiting period, or to `0` to stop all deletion.
- If you re-create a budget you deleted (upload it again under the same id), it keeps its documents and starts with no old shares.
- The server's `/metrics` shows how many bytes of documents are stored, how many are waiting to be deleted, and the result of the last cleanup.
- The hosted MCP server has a new `list_transaction_attachments` tool that lists a transaction's documents (metadata only; a split line lists its parent's). It treats attachment types and ids as untrusted.
- The iPhone app has a native Budget tab, behind a setting. You can edit a budgeted amount, step through months with arrows and a month menu, use tracking budgets (income Balance hidden, Saved or Overspent as on the web), and move money between categories and to-budget.

#### Enhancements

- Devices report removed and restored documents to the server when they sync.
- Pull down on the native Budget tab to sync, with a clear offline message when sync fails. The month menu knows the budget's range and puts a checkmark on the month you are viewing.
- The app has a new Home Economics icon, a jar of coins, on iPhone, Mac, Windows, Android and in the browser tab.
- The iPhone and Mac apps have a native look that follows the system's light and dark setting; the web app keeps its themes.
- The iPhone app uses the system tab bar for Budget, Accounts, Reports and Settings, with an Add button for new transactions and a light tap when you save a transaction or move money.
- In the iPhone app, attached documents open inside the app and can be saved to Photos, and signing out returns to the app's own sign-in screen.
- In the iPhone app, Back goes up one level, including on the Schedules list, and the phone layout holds in landscape.
- VoiceOver support on the native Budget screen, with an edit action on editable rows.
- The native bridge checks the page's origin before native code calls into it, so a foreign sign-in page can't be answered.

#### Bugfixes

- Changing the encryption key no longer fails when an attached document is missing from the server, and attached documents open again after another device changes the key.
- The desktop app removes the temporary copies of opened attachments when it quits.
- The iPhone and iPad app's content, and the iPad's top bar, stay clear of the status bar, camera cutout, rounded corners and home indicator.
- The iOS app launches when built with Xcode 27.

#### Maintenance

- No upstream changes in this release; the fork stays on upstream Actual 26.11.0.
- The hosted MCP server's dependencies `hono` and `@hono/node-server` are updated past security advisories.
- The rate-limit test is made tolerant of a loaded machine.
- Native iPhone and Mac app work and its OpenSpec records; the native app code itself affects iOS only.

#### Known limitations

- Experimental.
- Tapping a tag in the transaction editor does nothing.
- Tags are not yet fully accessible on the phone.
- OpenID sign-out needs the server's address in the identity provider's allowed sign-out addresses; without it the provider shows an error page, though you are already signed out of Actual.
- Attached documents aren't included in a budget export.
- Documents removed before this release are recorded the first time an updated device syncs, and deleted 30 days after that. Removals made on a device that has not been updated are not tracked.
- HEIC pictures are refused; use the Photos library or a JPEG or PNG copy.
- Attaching a document needs a connection; there is no offline upload queue.
- After restoring server data from a backup, pause the cleanup first (`production/RESTORE.md`).
