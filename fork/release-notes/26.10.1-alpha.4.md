# Release 26.10.1-alpha.4 — Ken’s Actual fork

October 27, 2026

**Unofficial fork prerelease.** Based on Actual 26.10.0 (`2bebdbaae0b701120a8cad9d56aafd64fa04c577`); not an official Actual Budget release.

Adds experimental business calendars, the first step toward paycheck scheduling. Turn on **Business calendars** under Settings → Experimental features, then open **Business calendars** on the desktop Schedules page.

Docker Tag: `ghcr.io/kenlefeb/actual-hosting:run-<successful workflow run>-1` (private; see the accompanying `image.ref`, `image.id` and `source.json` for exact provenance).

#### Features

- Define business calendars with non-working weekdays, holidays (fixed date, nth weekday or last weekday of a month) and per-year exceptions such as one-off closures.
- Choose how weekend holidays are observed: Saturday to Friday and Sunday to Monday, Sunday to Monday only, or not moved.
- Copy the U.S. Federal Holidays or U.S. Federal Reserve preset and edit it; preview each year's non-business days.

#### Enhancements

- Calendars are stored in the budget, sync between devices and support undo.

#### Bugfixes

- No fork bug fixes in this release; existing mobile and desktop note, split and sync improvements are retained.

#### Maintenance

- Record existing fork behavior as OpenSpec specifications and add tests for desktop note Enter handling and sync-disabled mobile status.
- Add a `business_calendars` table migration; existing budgets are unchanged until a calendar is created.
- Exclude OpenSpec-generated files from formatting checks.

#### Known limitations

- Experimental and off by default; calendars are managed on desktop only.
- Schedules and paychecks do not use calendars yet; that arrives in later releases.
- Holidays tied to Easter must be added as yearly exceptions.
- Synthetic staging only; no real financial data or production paperwork migration. No iPhone acceptance test is claimed.
