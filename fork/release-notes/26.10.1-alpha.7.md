# Release 26.10.1-alpha.7 — Ken’s Actual fork

October 27, 2026

**Unofficial fork prerelease.** Based on Actual 26.10.0 (`2bebdbaae0b701120a8cad9d56aafd64fa04c577`); not an official Actual Budget release.

Schedules can now skip holidays, not just weekends. With **Business calendars** turned on under Settings → Experimental features, a recurring schedule's "Move schedule before/after" option can use one of your business calendars, so a bill or paycheck due on a holiday moves to the nearest business day.

Docker Tag: `ghcr.io/kenlefeb/actual-hosting:run-<successful workflow run>-1` (private; see the accompanying `image.ref`, `image.id` and `source.json` for exact provenance).

#### Features

- Move a recurring schedule's dates before or after the non-business days of a chosen business calendar, such as U.S. Federal Holidays, instead of only weekends.

#### Enhancements

- Next dates, upcoming dates, account previews, posted transactions, forecasts and budget schedule templates all use the chosen calendar.
- The schedule's description names the calendar, for example "(before non-business days in U.S. Federal Holidays)".
- Editing or deleting a calendar updates the next dates of schedules that use it; undo restores both.

#### Bugfixes

- No fork bug fixes in this release; earlier improvements are retained.

#### Maintenance

- Record schedule business-day behavior as an OpenSpec specification with tests for date moves, previews, templates, the schedule editor and calendar changes.

#### Known limitations

- Experimental and off by default. If a schedule's calendar is deleted, it falls back to weekends and the editor says so.
- Matching bank-imported transactions to schedules is unchanged.
- Business calendars are managed on desktop only; mobile can choose a calendar in the schedule editor.
- Releases published on the same day may show the "What's new" message for any of them; the Notifications page lists all.
- Synthetic staging only; no real financial data or production paperwork migration. No iPhone acceptance test is claimed.
