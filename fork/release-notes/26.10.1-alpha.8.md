# Release 26.10.1-alpha.8 — Ken’s Actual fork

October 27, 2026

**Unofficial fork prerelease.** Based on Actual 26.10.0 (`2bebdbaae0b701120a8cad9d56aafd64fa04c577`); not an official Actual Budget release.

A schedule's date can now follow another schedule. With **Business calendars** turned on under Settings → Experimental features, choose "Relative to another schedule" as a schedule's date and set an offset, such as a deposit "2 business days before Payday".

Docker Tag: `ghcr.io/kenlefeb/actual-hosting:run-<successful workflow run>-1` (private; see the accompanying `image.ref`, `image.id` and `source.json` for exact provenance).

#### Features

- Date a schedule relative to another schedule: a number of days or business days before or after it, counted from the other schedule's moved date.

#### Enhancements

- Business days can follow a chosen business calendar, and the result can again be moved before or after non-business days.
- Next dates, upcoming dates, account previews, posted transactions, forecasts, budget templates and transaction matching all use relative dates.
- Descriptions read, for example, "2 business days before Payday (U.S. Federal Holidays)".
- Editing the other schedule or a business calendar updates relative schedules; undo restores both.
- If the other schedule is deleted, the relative schedule shows "Source schedule was deleted" until you choose another one or delete it.

#### Bugfixes

- No fork bug fixes in this release; earlier improvements are retained.

#### Maintenance

- Record relative schedule dates as an OpenSpec specification with tests for date math, matching, previews, the schedule editor and source changes.

#### Known limitations

- Experimental and off by default.
- A relative schedule cannot be the source of another relative schedule, and offsets go up to 366.
- Skipping or posting the source schedule does not skip or post relative schedules.
- If the source schedule is deleted on another device, relative schedules there update their next dates the next time they are recomputed.
- Releases published on the same day may show the "What's new" message for any of them; the Notifications page lists all.
- Synthetic staging only; no real financial data or production paperwork migration. No iPhone acceptance test is claimed.
