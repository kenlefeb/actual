# Release 26.10.1-alpha.9 — Ken’s Actual fork

October 27, 2026

**Unofficial fork prerelease.** Based on Actual 26.10.0 (`2bebdbaae0b701120a8cad9d56aafd64fa04c577`); not an official Actual Budget release.

Paychecks arrive. With **Business calendars** turned on under Settings → Experimental features, "Add paycheck" on the Schedules page defines gross pay, deductions and direct deposits once, and keeps the schedules for the pay date and each deposit in step.

Docker Tag: `ghcr.io/kenlefeb/actual-hosting:run-<successful workflow run>-1` (private; see the accompanying `image.ref`, `image.id` and `source.json` for exact provenance).

#### Features

- Define a paycheck: pay dates, gross pay and its income category, deductions to a category or as a transfer (such as a 401(k)), and direct deposits that are a fixed amount, a percent of net pay, or the remainder.
- Each paycheck books gross pay and deductions as one split transaction on the pay date in an on-budget clearing account, so the budget sees the full income and every deduction.
- Accounts get a "Business days" setting: the account's business calendar and how many business days early its direct deposits arrive.

#### Enhancements

- Each deposit is dated by its receiving account's early-deposit rule, counted in that account's calendar, and becomes a transfer from the clearing account.
- Bank-synced deposits link to their deposit schedules when the amount is close; "Post automatically" on a deposit adds it on its date instead, for manual accounts or steady amounts.
- "Skip next paycheck" skips the pay date and all its deposits together; editing, deleting and account changes keep the schedules in step, with undo.
- Saving any schedule no longer undoes a skip on schedules dated relative to it unless its dates change.

#### Bugfixes

- No fork bug fixes in this release; earlier improvements are retained.

#### Maintenance

- Record paychecks and account business days as OpenSpec specifications with tests for amounts, dates, posting, bank import matching, skipping and undo.

#### Known limitations

- Experimental and off by default; the paycheck editor is desktop only.
- Each deposit must go to a different account, and every paycheck repeats its defined amounts; edit a posted transaction for a one-off change.
- Imported deposits more than about 7.5% from the expected amount do not link automatically.
- Releases published on the same day may show the "What's new" message for any of them; the Notifications page lists all.
- Synthetic staging only; no real financial data or production paperwork migration. No iPhone acceptance test is claimed.
