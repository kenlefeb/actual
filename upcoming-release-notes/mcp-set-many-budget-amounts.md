Let AI assistants set many budget amounts at once in the MCP server, turn category carryover on or off, hold or release money for next month, copy last month, set a month to its 3, 6 or 12 month average (shown cell by cell first, and undoable), and apply or overwrite a month's budget templates (shown with their goals first, not undoable). The server's operator can change its per-call limits.
